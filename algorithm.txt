COMMUNITY WASTE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

ALGORITHM

1. Start.
2. Enter the resident’s name.
3. Enter the location of the waste.
4. Enter the type of waste.
5. Check whether all details are entered.
6. If any details are missing, ask the resident to enter them again.
7. If all details are complete, record the complaint.
8. Display a success message.
9. Ask if the resident wants to report another complaint.
10. If yes, repeat the process.
11. If no, display a thank-you message.
12. Stop.
